## Observations

- **Q5 fixed end-to-end at faith=1.0 / cite=✓.** This was the failure mode that motivated ADRs 0007 and the model swap. Qwen2.5 cites page 8 directly for the BLEU numbers; the grounding check has nothing to strip. Compare to the original Llama baseline where `cite=False` and `faith=1.000` was achieved by Llama spreading its cite to a *different* gold-relevant page — Qwen + grounding hits the *exact* must_cite page.
- **Latency improvement is the other quiet win.** The provider behind Qwen2.5 (`together`) consistently responds in <1 s; the previous Llama route through `novita` was averaging 3-8 s and frequently 504-ing on free-tier quotas. UX impact is bigger than the quality numbers suggest.
- **Q6 unchanged at recall=0.00.** The retrieval-side failure remains. ColPali (ADR-0006) is still the right next move; it requires GPU.

## What is left

- **Q6** is the only remaining hard failure. Recall=0.00 means the answerer never sees the right page. Pure retrieval problem; visual retrieval (ColPali, ADR-0006) is the planned fix.
- **Gold-set sharpening** (multi-page `must_cite_pages` instead of single `must_cite_page`) — would let `must_cite_rate` reach 1.0 on questions where multiple pages are defensibly correct.
